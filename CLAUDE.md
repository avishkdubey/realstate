# CLAUDE.md — Ahmedabad Real Estate Builder Website

Project brief + research dossier for a production-grade, high-performance, SEO-strong marketing
website for a **mid-market-to-premium real estate builder/developer in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India**.
Multi-project brand website (not a single-project microsite). Price bands ₹50 lakh–₹3+ crore
(apartments, villas, penthouses). Aesthetic: **"quiet luxury"** — restrained, editorial, cinematic
but fast. Lead handling is simple: enquiry forms + WhatsApp handoff to sales. **No CRM required.**

## 1. Market Context (research findings)

### National
- Premiumisation: homes >₹1Cr = **~78% of primary residential sales value** in 2025 across 50 cities
  (CREDAI–Liases Foras, 14 Mar 2026: 614,218 units, ₹8.46 lakh crore, +16% YoY value); ~50% of volume,
  up from 16% in early 2018.
- Affordable (<₹50L) collapsed to ~6% of new launches in Q2 2026 (from ~52% in 2018, ANAROCK).
  Average ticket size ~₹1.47 crore (India Housing Report 2026, CRE Matrix & NAR-INDIA).
- Consolidation: price growth moderated to ~6% in 2025 (from ~17% in 2024); volumes down ~12–14%.
  Unsold inventory +2.82% YoY to 509,815 units (Knight Frank); overhang concentrated in ₹1Cr+ stock.
- Rate tailwind: RBI repo cut 25bps to **5.25% on 5 Dec 2025** (125bps total in 2025). Home loans now
  **7.10–7.35%** (SBI/Union/BoB), down from 8.50–9.00%. Developers report 12–15% site-visit surge
  after favourable rate moves.
- NRI demand: international inflows +37% YoY in H1 2025 (~US$3.1B/yr); NRI share of purchases risen
  from ~7–10% (2015–18) to ~18–20% (2025). NRI luxury demand +38% YoY.

### Ahmedabad
- Outperformed India: **6,745 residential launches in Q1 2026, +30% QoQ / +29% YoY** (Cushman &
  Wakefield MarketBeat), ~31% above 3-yr quarterly average; West peripheral submarket leading.
  Q2 2026: 6,143 launches, +19% YoY; high-end + luxury ≈ 33% of new inventory (4× annual growth).
- Led India in prime luxury appreciation in 2025 (**14–22%**), ahead of Mumbai (9–14%) and
  Bengaluru (8–12%), yet ₹/sqft remains **40–60% below** equivalent Mumbai addresses.
- End-user dominated, lower volatility than Mumbai/Bengaluru. Mid-segment (₹70L–1.5Cr) ≈ 52% of market.
  Rental yields improved to 3–3.5%.
- GIFT City is the primary gravitational force: >25,000 professionals today, target 75,000 by 2028.
  Gujarati diaspora (US) holds an estimated US$80–100B in liquid wealth.

## 2. Audience & Psychology

**Segments:** local Gujarati business families (status, society reputation, joint-family space);
salaried professionals 25–40 (first-time, near employment hubs); NRI Gujaratis (US/UK/Canada/East
Africa); investors (plotted / GIFT corridor).

**Millennials + Gen Z** are 90–95% of purchases in major markets today and ~60% of new buyers by 2030;
36% of urban Gen Z actively consider ownership (Knight Frank). They:
- search **100% digitally**, bypassing brokers as first contact — a slow or dated site reads as poor
  construction quality and causes instant abandonment;
- demand transparent pricing *before* talking to sales, self-serve info, mobile-first;
- rely on credit (53% of home-loan applications); EMI-to-income near 40%, sometimes >50%; 70% of
  millennials fund deposits from equity/MF gains;
- prefer efficient, flexible layouts (hybrid work), larger balconies, natural light, smart storage;
- treat ESG/sustainability, app-based building management, EV charging, parcel rooms as baseline;
- distrust hard-sell; trust communities, reviews, real testimonials.

**Trust signals that work in India:** prominent RERA number, delivered-project track record, honest
on-time-possession claims, bank/home-loan tie-ups, **dated construction-progress photos**,
IGBC/GRIHA ratings, awards, family legacy ("since 19XX"), physical office address on every page,
real video testimonials, verified named quotes placed *next to* the form (never anonymous, never
buried in the footer).

**Vastu is a genuine decision driver.** East and North-East facing most prized; east-facing can carry
a 3–5% resale premium. Surface it: direction labels on floor plans, "Vastu-compliant" badges,
facing filters.

**Cultural drivers:** joint-family needs, "sapno ka ghar", society status, proximity to
schools/temples/community, resale + yield thinking, and **auspicious booking timing** — Navratri,
Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya, Uttarayan. Build a conditional festive-offer banner slot.

**WhatsApp is the conversion channel.** ~60–70% of Indian metro property enquiries arrive via
WhatsApp (Hyperleap AI, 2026); >98% open rate, most read within 3 minutes vs email's ~20%.
**Speed is the biggest lever:** replies within 5 minutes convert 60–75% to site visits vs 5–12% after
60+ minutes; leads contacted <5 min are ~21× more likely to convert than after 30 min.

## 3. Competitive Teardown

**Global luxury sites (what feels expensive):** scroll-as-storytelling; one hard idea executed
cleanly instead of stacked effects; WebGL for *atmosphere, not spectacle*; confident editorial
typography; weighted smooth scroll; invisible transitions. 2026 award winners have largely abandoned
heavy video backgrounds (<8%) and full-page hero-image-with-overlay (<12%).
Refs: Awwwards, FWA, CSS Design Awards, Godly, Lapa Ninja; Iventions, ERA/Silver Pinewood (Vide Infra).

**Indian developer sites (Lodha, Godrej, Sobha, DLF, Prestige, Oberoi, Hiranandani, Mahindra
Lifespaces, Rustomjee, Piramal, Total Environment, Embassy)**
- *Good:* strong brand hero, project filters, floor plans + virtual tours, sustainability messaging,
  testimonials, awards, legacy narrative.
- *Bad:* aggressive pop-up lead gates, forms demanding phone before showing anything, slow hero video,
  generic stock photography, no floor-plan zoom, no dated construction updates, no pricing
  transparency, broken mobile, template-y WordPress builds, weak local SEO.

**Portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, NoBroker, Square Yards):** buyers expect map search,
filters (BHK/budget/possession/RERA), EMI calculators, locality guides + price trends, verified
photos, instant contact. **Match or beat portals** on filtering, location intelligence, transparency
and speed — while delivering a brand experience portals cannot.

## 4. Tech Stack (all free / open-source)

- **Next.js 16** (App Router, TypeScript, React 19, Server Components by default, Turbopack).
  Typed routes are on by default — a `<Link>` to a route that does not exist fails the build,
  so stub pages must land before the links that point at them.
- **Tailwind CSS v4** + **shadcn/ui** (Radix primitives)
- **Framer Motion** (component reveals and micro-interactions) + **GSAP 3.13+** with ScrollTrigger
  (scroll-linked headline reveals) + **Lenis** (smooth scroll).
  Framer must be used through `LazyMotion` with the `domAnimation` feature set and the `m`
  primitive, never the full `motion` component — the difference is roughly 150–300ms of blocking
  time on pages carrying a dozen reveals. `components/motion/motion-provider.tsx` sets `strict`
  so a stray `motion` import fails loudly.
  - GSAP is **100% free since 30 Apr 2025** (Webflow announcement), including all former Club plugins,
    commercial use covered. No paid dependency remains in the motion stack.
- **React Three Fiber** + `@react-three/drei` + `@react-three/postprocessing` (tasteful WebGL only)
- **Embla Carousel** (galleries), **react-hook-form + Zod** (forms), **schema-dts** (typed JSON-LD)
- **Sanity CMS** (projects/units/amenities/locations/testimonials/blog), Studio embedded at `/studio`.
  Alternative: Payload v3 (self-host). Sanity recommended for a non-technical builder team.
- Email: **Resend** (fallback Nodemailer). Spam: **Cloudflare Turnstile** + honeypot
- Images/video: `next/image` + **ImageKit** (or Cloudinary) free tier
- Maps: **MapLibre GL** + OpenStreetMap (Google Maps only if reviews/embeds needed).
  Mounted on intersection behind a server-rendered SVG corridor map, so the ~200KB library never
  sits on the critical path and a blocked tile host still leaves a usable map.
  **Before launch:** set `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAP_TILE_URL` to a provider you hold an account with —
  OSM's public tiles are not licensed for production traffic.
- 360 tours: **Pannellum** (~21KB, MIT) — UMD, so imported inside an effect on intersection rather
  than at module scope. Alt: Marzipano, Photo Sphere Viewer
- Analytics: Vercel Analytics + GA4 via GTM; Meta Pixel for retargeting
- Hosting: **Vercel**. Bundle watch: `@next/bundle-analyzer`

## 6. Design System — "Quiet Luxury"

### Color (60-30-10)
```
--charcoal:   #141414    --charcoal-2: #1A1A1A    --ivory: #F5F1E8    --cream: #EDE7DA
--gold:       #B99C6B    --bronze:     #8C6A3F    --forest: #243B34
--grey:       #6B6B6B    --grey-2:     #9A9A9A
```
- **60% ground:** warm off-whites / ivory / stone grey / deep charcoal — let architecture dominate.
- **30% structural:** rich navy `#0A192F`, dark emerald `#043927` / forest `#243B34`, stone grey.
  Burgundy/plum optional for moody editorial.
- **10% accent:** muted metallics only — champagne/muted gold `#D4AF37`, soft bronze `#CD7F32`.
  Never saturated primaries, neon, or chaotic gradients (they read as *discount*).
- **Gold-on-charcoal small text must pass WCAG AA 4.5:1** — lighten to `#C9AE7C`+ if needed.
- **Bronze-on-ivory small text likewise.** Measured: `#8C6A3F` on `#F5F1E8` is 4.28:1 and fails.
  The implementation ships `#7A5A33` (5.5:1) as `--bronze`; use it for eyebrows and captions.

### Typography
- **Max two families.** Display serif = **Fraunces** (variable, Google Fonts) or Playfair Display /
  Cormorant. Body/UI = **Manrope** or **Inter**. Gujarati/Hindi = Noto Sans Gujarati / Devanagari.
- Self-host via `next/font` (subset). Fontshare fonts (Satoshi, General Sans) are free but under ITF
  EULA — **use their API, do not self-host without consent**.
- Type scale (1.25): **12, 14, 16 (base), 20, 25, 31, 39, 49, 61 px**. Line-height 1.5 body / 1.1 display.
- Generous letter-spacing (`tracking-widest`) on small uppercase nav/subheads — utility `.eyebrow`.
- Body line length constrained to **50–75 characters** (`max-w-prose`).
- Never system defaults (Arial) or decorative scripts.

### Space & geometry
- Spacing scale (4px base): 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. Grid: 12-col, max-w 1440px.
- **Whitespace is the luxury.** Double or triple template-standard margins (`py-24`, `gap-16`).
- **Every element aligns to the grid.** A 2–3px misalignment shatters the precision illusion.
- Radius restrained 2–8px. Elevation soft and low-contrast — prefer 1px borders + tonal shifts
  over heavy shadows.

### Imagery
- No cheap staged stock — HNW buyers detect it instantly. Artistic, candid, unique crops, dramatic
  architectural angles.
- **Color-grade all assets as one system** (identical lighting, shadow depth, temperature).
- If an image adds no emotional or informational value, delete it and leave negative space. Let images
  breathe; never edge-to-edge collages.
- Copy sells lifestyle, status and neighbourhood culture — minimal, sophisticated, zero hype. Lead
  with brand philosophy before dumping listings.

### Motion tokens
- Micro 150–200ms · UI 250–350ms · section reveals 400–600ms. **Never exceed 500ms** for interface
  animation; snappy beats slow.
- Easings: entrance `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`; transition `cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`;
  Framer default `[0.25,0.1,0.25,1]`.
- Animate composite properties only (opacity, transform) → 60fps.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for pinned/scroll-linked sequences + SplitText headline reveals; Framer Motion
  for component/page micro-interactions; Lenis (lerp ~0.1); View Transitions API for routes where
  supported.
- **Always honor `prefers-reduced-motion`** — disable Lenis, reduce GSAP to ≤300ms fades. Disable
  smooth scroll on touch if INP suffers.
- Converts: scroll-linked reveals, parallax within masks, horizontal galleries, sticky section
  transitions, tasteful text-mask reveals. Avoid: gimmicky cursors, motion that delays content,
  autoplay audio, chaotically spinning 3D models.

### Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard nav, visible focus, form labels, alt text, reduced-motion respected.

## 7. WebGL / Three.js Guardrails

Treat the viewport as a cinematic film set, not a toy.

- **Materials:** PBR `MeshStandardMaterial` with real roughness/metalness/normal maps (e.g.
  metalness 0.8 / roughness 0.2). Never flat unlit meshes.
- **Lighting:** architectural-studio setup — soft ambient occlusion, subtle directional light with
  shadow mapping (`<ambientLight intensity={0.5} />` + `<directionalLight castShadow />`).
- **Post-processing:** subtle SSR, controlled bloom, chromatic aberration, film grain, vignette.
- **Camera:** no jarring cuts or free-roam (motion sickness). Dampened lerp / GSAP drone-like reveals.
- **Budgets:** hero scene <100k triangles, <30 draw calls; DPR capped at 1.5.
- **Assets:** Draco/meshopt-compressed GLTF; KTX2 or WebP textures (GPU-direct, no CPU decode).
- **Memory:** never allocate inside `useFrame`/rAF (`new THREE.Vector3()` → GC stutter). Instantiate
  vectors/matrices once and recycle. Pause rAF when offscreen (IntersectionObserver) and on tab blur.
- **Threading:** offload the Three.js pipeline to a Web Worker via **OffscreenCanvas** so shader
  compilation/geometry parsing never blocks the main thread (keeps INP <200ms).
- **Loading:** wrap `<Canvas>` in `dynamic(() => import(...), { ssr:false })` — never block LCP.
  Reserve container dimensions with CSS `aspect-ratio` to prevent CLS.
- **Fallbacks:** mobile / low-power / reduced-motion get a high-res static WebP or `<video poster>`
  instead of the 3D scene.
- **Progressive enhancement is mandatory:** real H1, body copy and links live in server-rendered HTML
  behind/around the canvas. Bots read HTML, not WebGL pixels.

micro-market**, NAP consistency, active review generation. Keyword clusters: "3 BHK flats in Bopal
Ahmedabad", "new projects on SG Highway Ahmedabad", "luxury villas Shela".

**AEO/GEO:** publish `/llms.txt`; keep all factual content server-rendered (most AI crawlers don't run

## 12. Forms, WhatsApp & Conversion

- **EnquiryForm:** react-hook-form + Zod. Required: **name, phone**. Optional: email, project, budget,
  possession, "I am an NRI" toggle. **DPDP consent checkbox required.** Turnstile + honeypot.
  POST `/api/enquiry` → Resend email to sales → success screen hands off to WhatsApp.
- **Top-of-funnel variant (LeadCaptureForm):** only three fields — First Name, Email, Intent select
  ("I am an Investor" / "I am looking for a Home"). Cutting 4 fields to 3 has lifted conversion up to
  50%. Use **multi-step progressive forms** for budget/timeline detail after the first micro-commitment.
- Style inputs with **border-bottom only** (no heavy boxes) to keep the editorial look. Small trust
  line under submit: "All inquiries are strictly confidential."
- OTP only for high-intent actions (site-visit booking) — never as a content gate.
- **WhatsApp deep link:** `https://wa.me/{E164}?text={encodeURIComponent(msg)}`, per-project prefill:
  `Hi, I'm interested in {ProjectName} ({BHK}, {MicroMarket}). Please share pricing & availability.`
- Sticky mobile action bar (Call | WhatsApp | Enquire) + persistent WhatsApp bubble sitewide.

## 13. Legal & Compliance (MUST IMPLEMENT)

- **RERA Act §11(2):** every advertisement/prospectus/website must prominently mention the Authority's
  website and the registration number. → RERA number + `https://gujrera.gujarat.gov.in` on **every
  project page and in the footer**.
- **§59:** advertising/selling without registration → penalty up to **10% of estimated project cost**;
  continued default → up to 3 years' imprisonment and/or a further 10%.
- **§12:** promoter is liable to compensate/refund for false or misleading statements in ads —
  disclaimers do **not** waive this liability.
- **GujRERA Order No. 108 (effective 15 Jun 2025):** mandatory **QR code + unique registration number
  + GujRERA URL** in all advertisements/brochures/booking forms; font size ≥ the contact-number font;
  display block at top-right of the first page; video ads must show it ≥3 seconds. Non-compliance →
  action under §63. *Verify exact wording against the official GujRERA PDF before launch.*
- **Disclaimers:** "Artist's Impression", "Image for representational purpose only" on renders/plans;
  "Map not to scale — indicative only" on maps.
- **TCCCPR 2018 (+ Feb 2025 amendments):** commercial calling/SMS requires DLT registration + recorded
  consent (real estate firms are named Principal Entities); scrub against DND (1909); first violation
  can bar outgoing telecom resources for 15 days.
- **DPDP Act 2023 (+ DPDP Rules 2025):** forms collecting name/phone need a clear privacy notice and
  free, specific, informed, unambiguous consent (§6). Ship a privacy policy, consent checkbox, and a
  consent-withdrawal path. Penalties up to ₹250 crore. Full compliance deadline **13 May 2027**.
- **Never use fake or placeholder RERA numbers in demos** — use `RERA: DEMO-PENDING`.

**RERA 2.0 context:** 70% escrow, mandatory possession dates, five-year defect liability, delayed-
possession interest at SBI MCLR + 2%; 1.38 lakh complaints resolved (Economic Survey 2024–25).
